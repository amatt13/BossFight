# BossFight
[![.NET](https://github.com/amatt13/BossFight/actions/workflows/dotnet.yml/badge.svg?branch=main)](https://github.com/amatt13/BossFight/actions/workflows/dotnet.yml)

Welcome to my browser game!
Boss Fight is a cooperative game about killing monsters and unlocking new classes and cool loot. You play together with other online players, and help each other by casting buffs and dealing damage to the current monster. Every player faces the same monster and need to work together to overcome the challenges. Players can vote on increasing or decreasing the monster's difficulty. More difficult monsters unlocks new loot and more interesting fights. Once in a while, an epic boss will spawn, which will require a significant effort to overcome! These boss monsters may drop their epic weapons for you to use!

This is a hobby game primarily made by myself, and is mostly used as a way for me to explore html, css, and js as they are some tools that I'm not very familiar with, as I'm primarily a BE kind of guy. No fancy libraries, mostly "not-made-here" mentality :-)
Help me if you want! The issues are very non-intuitive as they are mostly used as a rough todo-list for me to use internally. But feel free to contact me if you want to participate.

The site is almost always up on http://bossfight.uk.to/
